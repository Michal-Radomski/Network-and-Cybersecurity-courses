## Wireless Security Monitoring

**Wireless Security Monitoring** refers to the continuous observation, analysis, and protection of a wireless network
environment (WLAN) to detect and neutralize potential threats, unauthorized devices, and policy violations. Because Wi-Fi
signals travel through the air beyond physical walls, traditional firewalls cannot see what happens on the radio frequency
(RF) spectrum. Security monitoring fills this gap.

---

## Core Objectives of Monitoring

- **Real-Time Threat Detection:** Instantly spot malicious activities like Evil Twins, de-authentication floods, or
  unauthorized sniffing attempts.
- **Rogue Asset Discovery:** Detect unauthorized access points plugged into the corporate network or unauthorized ad-hoc
  connections created by users.
- **Compliance Assurance:** Meet regulatory frameworks (such as PCI-DSS for credit card processing or HIPAA for healthcare)
  that mandate continuous wireless scanning.

---

## How Wireless Security Monitoring Works

Continuous monitoring systems typically rely on dedicated hardware sensors or smart access points to listen to radio
channels. The monitoring framework generally performs four primary steps:

1. **Spectrum Scanning:** Continuously sweeps the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz frequency bands to capture management, control,
   and data frames.
2. **Analysis and Classification:** Compares captured traffic against known signatures, authorized device lists, and security
   policies to categorize the device or behavior (e.g., distinguishing between a friendly neighbor's Wi-Fi and a malicious
   rogue AP).
3. **Alerting (WIDS):** Logs the event and notifies administrators via dashboard alerts or emails if a suspicious anomaly or
   policy violation is identified.
4. **Mitigation (WIPS):** Automatically executes countermeasures—such as blocking rogue connections or dropping malicious
   frames—if configured with prevention capabilities.

---

## Deployment Architectures

Organizations typically choose from three main deployment models to implement wireless security monitoring:

- **Overlay Monitoring:** Uses dedicated wireless sensors (Air Monitors) placed throughout a facility strictly for monitoring
  purposes. They do not serve regular clients, providing uninterrupted, 24/7 multi-channel threat analysis.
- **Integrated Monitoring:** Configures existing access points to split their time or use dedicated dual radios so they can
  serve regular clients while simultaneously scanning the airwaves for security threats.
- **Hybrid Monitoring:** Combines both dedicated sensors in high-risk zones and integrated access points elsewhere to balance
  cost and security coverage.
