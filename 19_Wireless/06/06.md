### SOHO (Small Office/Home Office) Best Practices

Implementing strong **SOHO best practices** ensures that a small or home-based network remains secure, efficient, and
reliable. Because these environments often blend personal and professional devices, establishing clear boundaries and
hardened security measures is essential.

---

### 1. Network Security & Router Hardening

Your router is the gateway to your network. Securing it properly prevents unauthorized access from the outside.

- **Change Default Credentials:** Never leave the router's default administrator username and password (like
  `admin`/`password`). Change them immediately to unique, strong passphrases.
- **Update Firmware:** Enable automatic firmware updates or regularly check for patches to protect against newly discovered
  security vulnerabilities.
- **Use Modern Encryption:** Ensure your Wi-Fi uses **WPA3** encryption (or WPA2-AES at a minimum if older devices require
  it). Disable vulnerable legacy setups like WPS (Wi-Fi Protected Setup).
- **Disable Remote Management:** Turn off remote router administration over the internet unless absolutely necessary, so
  management is restricted to local wired or wireless connections.

---

### 2. Network Segmentation

Dividing your network into distinct zones prevents a compromised device (like a smart home appliance) from giving an attacker
access to your critical work files.

- **Create a Guest Network:** Keep visitors and clients on an isolated guest Wi-Fi network so they cannot see or access your
  primary network devices.
- **Isolate IoT Devices:** Smart speakers, thermostats, and other Internet of Things (IoT) devices have weaker security;
  place them on a separate VLAN or guest network away from your workstations.
- **Separate Work and Personal Use:** If possible, keep professional tasks strictly on company-issued or isolated work
  devices rather than blending them with personal family computers.

---

### 3. Authentication & Access Control

Strong identity management stops credential stuffing and unauthorized entry.

- **Strong Passwords and Passphrases:** Use long, unique passphrases for your Wi-Fi network and all administrative accounts.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Enable MFA on every work account, cloud service, email portal, and router interface
  that supports it.
- **The Principle of Least Privilege:** Ensure users only have access to the specific files, folders, and systems required to
  do their jobs.

---

### 4. Secure Remote Access & Data Protection

When working remotely or connecting back to a central office, data must be protected in transit and at rest.

- **Use a VPN (Virtual Private Network):** Always use a secure corporate VPN when accessing internal company servers or
  sensitive client data over public or home internet connections.
- **Regular Backups:** Implement automated, regular backups (such as a 3-2-1 backup strategy: 3 copies of data, across 2
  different media types, with 1 copy stored offsite or in the cloud) to protect against ransomware or hardware failure.
- **Endpoint Protection:** Keep up-to-date antivirus, anti-malware, and endpoint detection software active on all computers.

---

### 5. Physical Security

SOHO environments often lack enterprise-grade physical security, meaning physical safeguards must be managed manually.

- **Positioning Equipment:** Place work monitors and screens away from ground-floor windows where passersby can view
  sensitive information.
- **Clean Desk Policy:** Lock or log out of your computer whenever you step away from your workstation, especially if other
  household members or visitors are present.
- **Secure Disposal:** Shred sensitive physical documents and securely wipe or destroy old digital storage drives before
  recycling them.
