### What is a Remote Access Point (RAP)?

A **Remote Access Point (RAP)** is a specialized wireless access point designed to extend an organization's corporate network
securely to remote locations, such as an employee's home office, a temporary workspace, or a small branch office.

Unlike a standard access point that relies on being plugged directly into a local switch, a RAP connects to the internet and
automatically establishes a secure, encrypted tunnel back to the main corporate firewall or controller.

---

### How Remote Access Points Work

- **Automatic Tunneling:** When plugged into a home router or internet connection, the RAP reaches out over the public
  internet to the corporate network, establishing a Virtual Private Network (VPN) tunnel (such as IPsec) automatically.
- **Zero-Touch Provisioning:** RAPs are typically pre-configured by IT. An employee simply plugs the device into power and an
  ethernet port, and it downloads its corporate configuration without manual setup.
- **Corporate SSID Extension:** The RAP broadcasts the exact same corporate Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and security settings
  used at the main headquarters, making the user's devices feel as though they are sitting right inside the office.

---

### Key Benefits

- **Seamless Security:** All wireless traffic from the remote location is encrypted and sent directly through the corporate
  firewall, ensuring company security policies, content filtering, and monitoring apply even outside the office.
- **Centralized Management:** IT administrators can monitor performance, push firmware updates, and troubleshoot connectivity
  issues from a central dashboard.
- **Resource Access:** Employees can seamlessly access internal company resources, file shares, and printers without needing
  to manually launch a software VPN client every time.
