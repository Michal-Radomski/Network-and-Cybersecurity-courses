The **Wi-Fi Alliance** is a non-profit trade association that certifies Wi-Fi products for interoperability, security, and
performance. While technical protocols are defined by the **IEEE** (under the 802.11 standards), the Wi-Fi Alliance tests and
certifies hardware to ensure devices from different manufacturers seamlessly work together.

---

## Key Wi-Fi Generations (IEEE Standards vs. Wi-Fi Alliance Naming)

To simplify consumer marketing, the Wi-Fi Alliance introduced consumer-friendly naming conventions starting in 2018:

| Generation   | IEEE Standard   | Release Year | Frequency Bands       | Max Link Rate |
| ------------ | --------------- | ------------ | --------------------- | ------------- |
| **Wi-Fi 7**  | 802.11be        | 2024         | 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz | ~46 Gbps      |
| **Wi-Fi 6E** | 802.11ax (ext.) | 2021         | 6 GHz                 | ~9.6 Gbps     |
| **Wi-Fi 6**  | 802.11ax        | 2019         | 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz        | ~9.6 Gbps     |
| **Wi-Fi 5**  | 802.11ac        | 2014         | 5 GHz                 | ~6.9 Gbps     |
| **Wi-Fi 4**  | 802.11n         | 2009         | 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz        | ~600 Mbps     |

## Core Certification Programs & Protocols

Beyond standard generational throughput, the Wi-Fi Alliance oversees certification programs for security, connection ease,
and specialized features:

### 1. Security Standards

- **WPA3™ (Wi-Fi Protected Access 3):** The current benchmark for wireless security. Features enhanced encryption
  (128-bit/192-bit) and protection against dictionary-based password attacks using Simultaneous Authentication of Equals
  (SAE).
- **WPA2™:** The previous long-standing security standard using AES encryption.

### 2. Device Connectivity & Convenience

- **Wi-Fi Direct®:** Enables devices to connect directly with one another without needing a central wireless access point or
  router.
- **Wi-Fi Easy Connect™:** Simplifies onboarding smart home (IoT) devices using QR code scanning or NFC instead of manually
  typing passwords.
- **Wi-Fi Protected Setup (WPS):** Legacy standard designed to allow quick PIN or button-press connections (largely
  superseded due to security vulnerabilities).

### 3. Roaming & Performance Enhancements

- **Wi-Fi Passpoint® (Hotspot 2.0):** Allows devices to automatically and securely connect to public Wi-Fi networks without
  requiring manual sign-in credentials each time.
- **Wi-Fi Agile Multiband™:** Helps devices steer smoothly between different access points and frequency bands to optimize
  network coverage and capacity.
- **Miracast®:** A standard for streaming video and audio wirelessly from phones, tablets, or laptops to TVs and displays.
