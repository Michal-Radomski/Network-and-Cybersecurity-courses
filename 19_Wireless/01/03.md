**VoLTE** stands for **Voice over Long-Term Evolution**.

In simple terms, it is the technology that allows you to make traditional voice phone calls over a **4G LTE data network**,
rather than relying on older 2G or 3G cellular networks.

---

## How It Works

Before VoLTE, 4G networks were used almost exclusively for internet data (browsing, streaming, apps). When you made or
received a phone call, your phone would silently drop its connection down to a 3G or 2G network just to process the voice
signal.

VoLTE changes this by treating your voice call as standard digital data packets—similar to how VoIP services like FaceTime,
WhatsApp, or Skype work—allowing the call to stay entirely on the high-speed 4G network.

---

## Key Benefits of VoLTE

- **Superior Audio Quality:** VoLTE uses "HD Voice" technology, which captures a wider range of audio frequencies. Calls
  sound noticeably clearer, richer, and less muffled compared to older networks.
- **Faster Connection Times:** Calls set up much faster—often connecting in just 1 to 2 seconds, compared to the 5 to 10
  seconds typical of older 2G/3G fallbacks.
- **Simultaneous Voice and Data:** Because your phone stays on 4G during a call, you can talk on the phone while using
  high-speed mobile data at the same time (e.g., searching for directions while chatting).
- **Better Battery Life:** Constantly switching between 4G for data and 3G/2G for calls drains battery life. Keeping
  everything on 4G saves power.
- **Wi-Fi Calling Support:** VoLTE infrastructure makes it seamless for your phone to hand off a call between cellular 4G and
  a Wi-Fi network without dropping the call.

---

## Do You Need to Enable It?

Most modern smartphones (iPhones and Android devices released in the last several years) have VoLTE enabled by default, and
major cellular carriers require it—especially as legacy 2G and 3G networks are being phased out globally.

> **Tip:** You can usually check or toggle this setting on your phone under **Settings > Cellular / Mobile Data > Cellular
> Data Options** (iOS) or **Settings > Connections / Mobile Networks** (Android).
