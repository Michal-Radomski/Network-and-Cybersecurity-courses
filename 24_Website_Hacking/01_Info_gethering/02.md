### What is a WHOIS Lookup?

A **WHOIS lookup** is a query and response protocol used to query databases that store the registered users or assignees of
an Internet resource, such as a domain name, an IP address block, or an autonomous system. In the context of website
reconnaissance, pentesters use WHOIS queries to gather registration and administrative data associated with a target domain.

---

### Key Information Found in a WHOIS Record

When a pentester performs a WHOIS lookup on a domain, the returned record typically includes:

- **Registrar Information:** The company or registrar through which the domain name was purchased and registered (e.g.,
  GoDaddy, Namecheap, MarkMonitor).
- **Registration Dates:** Important timestamps showing when the domain was created, when it was last updated, and when it is
  scheduled to expire. Expired or newly created domains can sometimes indicate temporary, staging, or poorly monitored
  infrastructure.
- **Contact Information:** Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses for the registrant, administrative
  contact, and technical contact.
- **Name Servers:** The DNS servers responsible for resolving the domain name, which can point toward the hosting provider or
  cloud service being utilized.

---

### Why Pentesters Use WHOIS Lookups

1. **Mapping Corporate Ownership:** Pentesters can confirm whether a domain officially belongs to the target organization or
   a third-party vendor, subsidiaries, or partner companies.
2. **Finding Additional Assets:** Historical WHOIS data or overlapping administrative emails/phone numbers can help link
   multiple disparate domains back to the same parent organization, expanding the scope of the assessment.
3. **Identifying Privacy Protections:** Many organizations use domain privacy services to mask personal or corporate details.
   Finding that privacy protection is disabled can accidentally expose direct employee emails or internal phone numbers,
   which can later be used in social engineering or phishing simulations.

---

> **Note on Privacy:** Due to privacy regulations like GDPR, much of the contact information in modern WHOIS lookups is often
> redacted or anonymized unless queried by authorized entities or security researchers with a legitimate legal basis.
