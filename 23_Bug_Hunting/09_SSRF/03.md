The address **`[http://127.1/](http://127.1/)`** (which is a shortened version of **`127.0.0.1`**) is known as the **loopback
address** or **localhost**.

Here is what it means in simple terms:

- **Talking to Yourself:** In computer networking, this address always refers to the exact device you are currently using.
  When your device connects to `127.0.0.1`, it is essentially communicating with itself rather than sending data out to the
  wider internet.
- **Web Development:** Programmers and web developers frequently use `localhost` to test websites, apps, or servers locally
  on their own computers before publishing them online where other people can see them.

If you type this address into your web browser, you will usually see an error message (like "Unable to connect") unless you
happen to have a local web server actively running on your device.
