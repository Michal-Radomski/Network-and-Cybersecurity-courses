### What is FamilySearch.org?

**FamilySearch.org** is a free, non-profit genealogy website and research platform operated by **The Church of Jesus Christ
of Latter-day Saints** (commonly known as the LDS Church). It is one of the largest and most popular genealogy databases in
the world, helping millions of people research their family history and build family trees.

---

### Key Features and Services

- **Global Records Database:** The site hosts billions of digitized historical records from all over the world, including
  census data, birth, marriage, and death certificates, immigration and naturalization papers, military records, and
  probate/land documents.
- **The Shared Family Tree:** Unlike many platforms where every user maintains a private, isolated tree, FamilySearch
  features a massive, crowdsourced **Family Tree**. Users collaborate to build a single, global tree for humanity, adding
  ancestors, sources, and biographical details that others can view and contribute to.
- **Free Access:** Unlike commercial competitors (such as Ancestry.com), FamilySearch is **100% free** to use. Anyone can
  create an account and access its extensive records without a paid subscription.
- **Research Wiki and Help:** The platform provides a comprehensive "Research Wiki" that offers step-by-step guides, search
  strategies, and record availability breakdowns by country, state, and region.
- **FamilySearch Centers:** The organization operates physical research facilities (including the famous Family History
  Library in Salt Lake City, Utah, and smaller local centers worldwide) where visitors can access restricted digital records
  and get free help from trained volunteers.
