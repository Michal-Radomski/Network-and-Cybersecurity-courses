**Phone Number OSINT** (Open Source Intelligence) is the practice of gathering publicly available information linked to a
specific phone number.

Because phone numbers are often tied to online accounts, personal records, and business listings, OSINT researchers,
cybersecurity professionals, and investigators use these techniques to verify identities or assess security risks.

---

## 🔍 Key Information Revealed by OSINT

When analyzing a phone number, investigators look for several layers of data:

### 1. Carrier & Line Data

- **Carrier Name:** Identifies the network service provider (e.g., Verizon, Vodafone, Orange).
- **Line Type:** Distinguishes between landlines, mobile numbers, and **VoIP** (Voice over IP, like Google Voice or Skype).
- **Location:** Identifies the country code, region, or city associated with the number's area code.
- **Porting History:** Shows if the number was transferred from one provider or line type to another.

### 2. Digital Footprint & Account Associations

- **Social Media Accounts:** Many platforms allow users to find contacts by phone number or use numbers for account recovery.
- **Messaging Apps:** Platforms like WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, or Viber confirm whether a number is active on their network
  and may reveal profile photos or display names.
- **Breach Data:** Checking if the number appears in public data breaches to identify linked email addresses or usernames.

### 3. Public Records & Business Listings

- **Online Listings:** Matches the number against yellow pages, business websites, classified ads, or domain registration
  (WHOIS) records.
- **Caller ID Databases:** Cross-referencing community-driven spam identification databases.

## 🛠️ Common Tools & Frameworks

| Tool Type                   | Examples                                    | Purpose                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lookup APIs**             | Twilio Lookup, Numverify                    | Identifies carrier, line type, country, and formatting.                                 |
| **Specialized OSINT Tools** | PhoneInfoga, Ignorant                       | Automates searching across search engines, international formats, and social platforms. |
| **Reverse Search Engines**  | Google, DuckDuckGo, Specialized Directories | Locates web pages, forum posts, or public documents referencing the number.             |

---

## 🛡️ Practical Applications & Ethics

- **Cybersecurity & Fraud Prevention:** Used by defenders to verify transactions, detect spam callers, or investigate
  phishing attempts.
- **Journalism & Fact-Checking:** Used to verify the identity of sources or contact details.
- **Privacy Risks:** Phone numbers serve as strong unique identifiers. Because of this, exposing a phone number online makes
  individuals vulnerable to SIM swapping, targeted phishing (smishing), or unwanted tracking.
