Filtering posts on X (formerly Twitter) can be done using either the built-in graphical interface or by typing specific text
commands (called search operators) directly into the search bar.

---

### Method 1: Using the Advanced Search Interface (Desktop Browser)

If you are using X on a desktop web browser, you can use the built-in form:

1. Go to **`x.com/search-advanced`** (or click the **Advanced search** option on the search results page).
2. A menu will pop up allowing you to fill in specific fields, such as:

- **Words:** Find posts with all words, exact phrases, or specific hashtags.
- **Accounts:** Filter posts sent _from_, _to_, or _mentioning_ specific usernames.
- **Dates:** Select a specific timeframe (`from` and `to` dates).
- **Engagements:** Set minimum thresholds for likes, replies, or reposts.

3. Click **Search** to apply the filters.

---

### Method 2: Using Search Operators (Desktop or Mobile)

You can type commands directly into the standard search bar on both desktop and mobile to filter posts instantly.

#### 1. Account Filters

- **`from:username`** — Shows posts posted only by that specific account (e.g., `from:NASA`).
- **`to:username`** — Shows replies sent to a specific account.
- **`@username`** — Shows posts that mention an account.

#### 2. Keyword & Content Filters

- **`"exact phrase"`** — Puts quotes around words to search for an exact phrase match (e.g., `"space exploration"`).
- **`-word`** — Uses a minus sign to exclude posts containing a specific word (e.g., `galaxy -mars`).
- **`word1 OR word2`** — Searches for either term (the `OR` must be capitalized).

#### 3. Media & Link Filters

- **`filter:media`** — Shows only posts containing images or videos.
- **`filter:images`** — Shows only posts containing images.
- **`filter:videos`** — Shows only posts containing videos.
- **`filter:links`** — Shows only posts that include a URL.

#### 4. Date Filters

- **`since:YYYY-MM-DD`** — Shows posts published on or after a specific date (e.g., `since:2026-01-01`).
- **`until:YYYY-MM-DD`** — Shows posts published before a specific date.

#### 5. Engagement Filters

- **`min_faves:NUMBER`** — Filters for posts with at least a certain number of likes (e.g., `min_faves:100`).
- **`min_retweets:NUMBER`** — Filters for posts with at least a certain number of reposts.
- **`min_replies:NUMBER`** — Filters for posts with at least a certain number of replies.

---

### Example of Combining Filters

You can combine multiple commands in the search bar to narrow down results. For example:

> `space exploration since:2026-01-01 filter:media min_faves:50`

This query searches for posts about _space exploration_, published in 2026, that include media and have at least 50 likes.
