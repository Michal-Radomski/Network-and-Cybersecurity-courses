**SOCMINT** stands for **Social Media Intelligence**. It refers to the practice of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting
data from social media platforms (such as X, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook) to derive insights, track trends, or
support investigations.

It is considered a specialized sub-discipline of **OSINT** (Open Source Intelligence), which focuses broadly on any
information that is freely available to the public.

---

### Key Components of SOCMINT

SOCMINT looks at three main types of data on social platforms:

- **Profile Information:** Static details shared by users, such as bios, job titles, usernames, and profile pictures.
- **Interactions:** Public actions like posts, comments, replies, likes, shares, and the connections between different user
  accounts.
- **Metadata:** Contextual clues attached to content, such as timestamps, device information, and geotags (location data).

---

### Common Applications

- **Security and Law Enforcement:** Agencies use SOCMINT to gather leads, establish timelines, track threats, or locate
  missing persons by analyzing public social signals.
- **Business and Marketing:** Companies use "social listening" tools to monitor brand reputation, analyze public sentiment
  regarding products, track consumer trends, and screen job applicants using professional networks like LinkedIn.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Financial institutions and security teams analyze social footprints to help verify identities and
  spot fraudulent activities or fake accounts.
